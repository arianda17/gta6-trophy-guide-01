Offline static web app: a GTA VI trophy guide and tracker for all 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies. Includes per-trophy guides with sources, filters by grade and type, text search, check/uncheck tracking saved in localStorage, export/import of progress as JSON, and a spoiler toggle that hides story trophies until earned. Roadmap to the platinum and the single missable trophy (#28 Positive Mental Attitude) flagged.

Run locally: `node serve.ts` then open http://localhost:8080.
