Roadmap to the Platinum

  1. Story first. Play through the campaign. The chapter and mission trophies pop automatically and cannot be missed.
  2. The one missable. Trophy #28 Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house disappears after Chapter 1 and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required. Do it early, right after Down to Earth, before it is gone.
  3. Free roam. With both characters unlocked (after Coffee Break), work the collectibles, activities and encounters: WAiNK scans, vehicles, stunts, card sets, garages, challenges, gangs, bank vaults, races.
  4. The grind. The 100% completion gold, stunts, challenges, laundered money, spent money and 500 criminal-profile actions are the long tail. The platinum is earned in one playthrough; nothing is online-only.

This roadmap is built from the official trophy list's names, descriptions and PSN targets. Guide text says plainly where a method is not pinned down.
